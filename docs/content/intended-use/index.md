# Intended Use and Limitations

This release supports time-series forecasting, classification, and regression within the capabilities described in the documentation. High-risk applications under Article 6 of the EU AI Act are outside the intended purpose specified by NXAI. NXAI has not validated this release for compliance with the requirements applicable to a specific high-risk AI system.

Users and integrators must assess the suitability of the model for their application and comply with the legal obligations applicable to their role. Uses prohibited by applicable law, including Article 5 of the EU AI Act, are not permitted.

The full notice covers the intended purpose, excluded uses, model limitations, and integration responsibilities: [TiRex Intended Use, Limitations and EU AI Act Notice](https://github.com/NX-AI/tirex/blob/main/INTENDED_USE.md). This notice does not amend the applicable licence or displace any party's statutory obligations.
